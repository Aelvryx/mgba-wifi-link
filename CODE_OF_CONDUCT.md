# Code of conduct

Be kind, specific, and technically honest.

Project spaces must remain welcoming regardless of experience, identity,
background, or communication style. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Critique code, evidence, and decisions—not people. Assume good faith, correct
mistakes plainly, and give maintainers room to keep an experimental project
appropriately scoped.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or
its participants. Serious or sensitive conduct concerns can be raised through
GitHub's private security-reporting channel.
